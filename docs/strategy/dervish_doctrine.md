# Dervish Doctrine — Remember Gordon!

The Khalifa's playbook: numbers, melee, and the clock. The Dervish win by
British losses — 3 VP per AE land unit, 10 per leader, 10 per gunboat (§9.14) —
so forcing the AE into expensive contact is the whole game. Every entry cites
the manual section it rests on.

## Fire — use your few guns

1. **You win in melee, not at fire.** The Dervish get +2 melee vs the AE's +1
   (§7.7). At fire, the AE adds +1 direct-fire accuracy on top (§6.24) and can
   stack brigade-integrity +1 (§5.54). Do not trade long-range fire on even
   terms — close to melee where your edge lives.
   — §7.7, §6.24, §5.54

2. **Your artillery and gunboats fire on the artillery line; Jehadia, Danagla,
   and Isa Zachneih on the rifles line; everyone else is spears and swords**
   (§2.31). Direct fire with the spear-armed mass is weak — spend the turn
   moving them into melee range instead.
   — §2.31

3. **Dervish artillery is your only answer to gunboats and forts.** Only
   artillery may fire at gunboats (sink on CRT 3+) or forts (eliminate on CRT
   2+) (§6.61, §6.62). Your three artillery units are the only things on your
   side that can threaten a gunboat — keep them where they can reach the Nile.
   — §6.61, §6.62

## Melee and the advance

4. **The mandatory advance is your breakthrough tool.** When Dervish melee
   eliminates all defenders, *all* surviving eligible adjacent Dervish units
   MUST advance into the vacated hex, up to the stacking limit (§7.6). Engineer
   a two-hex-wide hit so the advance floods the gap instead of stacking one hex.
   Your offensive *fire* also opens advances: when your shots empty an enemy
   hex, your eligible firers may `AdvanceAfterCombat` into it that same phase
   (§6.82) — free ground toward the Tomb, no movement points spent.
   — §7.6, §5.51, §6.82

5. **Don't advance across walls or khors.** Advance after combat is forbidden
   across a wall hexside (except at a gate or breach) and across a khor (§6.82).
   Walls and khors channel the Dervish — mass at the gates and breaches, or
   bring artillery to open them (§6.63).
   — §6.82, §6.63

6. **Cavalry and camel units run fast and retreat once.** Cavalry/camel units
   may retreat two hexes from an infantry melee attack, once per turn (§7.5).
   Use them as the mobile arm: probe, withdraw, and refuse the AE's melee
   contact.
   — §7.5

## Position and terrain

7. **Forts hold the line — put them where the AE must pass.** A fort fires even
   alone (§6.54), gives −3 defensive to fire on units stacked inside it
   (§6.54), projects a ZOC even when unoccupied (§5.44), and cannot be entered
   by the AE (§6.54). Forts placed across the AE's approach corridor (south of
   the Khor Shambat on the west bank, or south of the Halfaya huts on the east
   bank — §9.111) channel and bleed the advance. The AE must destroy them with
   artillery (§6.62), melee (§7.6), or Royal Engineers (§6.53) — none of which
   is free.
   — §6.54, §5.44, §9.111, §6.62

8. **Guard the Khalifa — he is 10 VP.** The Khalifa is worth 10 VP if
   eliminated (§9.14). He and the Taiasha set up in the walled city (§9.111)
   and only the Khalifa, the Dervish artillery, and the Taiasha may enter the
   walled city (§5.23). Keep the Taiasha bodyguard stacked with him and keep
   the walls intact (see §7).
   — §9.14, §9.111, §5.23

9. **Isa Zachneih is the east-bank cork — keep it alive.** Eliminating Isa
   Zachneih is 1 VP to the AE *and* unlocks the Friendlies river crossing
   (§5.21, §9.14). While Zachneih lives, the AE cannot transport the Friendlies
   to the west bank (§5.21) — the east bank stays quiet. This unit is worth
   more than its 1 VP: keep it screened and out of artillery range.
   — §5.21, §9.14

10. **Screen with ZOC, but keep the screen undisrupted.** Every AE unit must
    stop the moment it enters a Dervish ZOC (§5.26, §5.43); a disrupted unit
    projects none (§5.41). Tribal infantry as a screening crust slows the
    whole AE army — but a disrupted screen is a highway, so rotate it.
    — §5.43, §5.41

11. **Respect stacking tribes.** Units of different Dervish tribes may not
    stack together (§5.52), and a leader may stack only with his own command
    (§5.53). Build each attack wave from one tribe plus its leader or the
    stacks come apart in the assault.
    — §5.52, §5.53

## Reinforcements and desertion

12. **Reinforcements arrive by the wave.** Dervish reinforcements enter the
    west edge south of the Khor Shambat, paying the terrain cost of the entry
    hex (§9.112): turn 1 the Baggara/Jaalin/Danagla/Kehena/Degheim and YAKUB,
    SHERIF, ALI WAD HELU; turn 2 the Hadendowa and OSMAN DIGNA; turn 3 the
    Mulazmin/Jehadia and SHEIK EL DIN (§9.112). Feed each wave straight into
    the melee line.
    — §9.112

13. **Choose deserters to keep your guns and elites.** The desertion roll
    removes 1½× the roll of units of the Dervish's choosing; the Khalifa,
    gunboats, artillery, and forts may not be chosen and no AE VP are awarded
    (§8.2). Shed the weakest tribal infantry, keep the artillery that threatens
    gunboats.
    — §8.2

## Optional rules (§10)

14. **Mines deny the Nile.** Lay up to two mines south of the Khor Shambat
    hexrow (not in the same hex) (§10.11): a British gunboat striking one rolls
    — 1–4 no effect, 5–7 engines lost (drift two hexes/turn), 8–10 sunk (§10.12).
    Your own gunboats pass freely (§10.14). Mining the approaches the AE
    gunboats must take turns a river lane into a lottery.
    — §10.11, §10.12, §10.14

15. **The chain cuts the river until the AE works for it.** A chain of up to
    four Nile hexes stops any gunboat entering (all boats) until sunk by AE
    infantry/cavalry spending a full turn adjacent on a bank, or by artillery
    scoring 3+ (§10.21–10.23). A chain + mines can paralyse the AE's naval
    arm — at the cost of one option slot (§10 opening note).
    — §10.21, §10.22, §10.23

## The clock

16. **Attrition favours you — the AE must come to you.** The AE needs the
    Mahdi's Tomb (25 VP) for a decisive result and must force a fight (§9.14);
    you score 3 VP for every AE land unit killed (§9.14). Let the AE bleed on
    your forts and ZOC screens before committing to the decisive melee.
    — §9.14
