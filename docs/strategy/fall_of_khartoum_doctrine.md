# Fall of Khartoum Doctrine — Remember Gordon!

Scenario deltas for the bonus game (§9.3). Both sides play by the Dervish Range
Effects Table (§9.343); turn 1 is always night (§9.341); every hex is playable
including half-hexes (§9.342). This file overlays the side doctrines — read it
with the faction file for the complete brief.

## Victory is the clock (§9.35)

1. **The Dervish win by killing GORDON — speed decides.** Dervish decisive:
   eliminate GORDON by turn 4; tactical: turn 5; marginal: turn 6. British
   marginal: GORDON survives turn 6; tactical: turn 7; decisive: turn 8
   (§9.35). Every Dervish turn wasted lets the level slip — drive at the palace
   from the first move.
   — §9.35

2. **Losses downgrade the Dervish victory.** The Dervish drop one level for
   16–23 units lost, two for 24–31, three for 32+ (§9.35). The AE can "win" a
   lost game simply by killing Dervish units — force the Dervish to spend
   units, and the Dervish must not spend them carelessly on the way to the
   palace.
   — §9.35

3. **GORDON only dies in the palace.** GORDON is fixed in the palace, may not
   move, and is eliminated only by a Dervish unit passing through or occupying
   the palace hex — as normal movement or advance after combat (§9.346). The
   palace is the single Dervish objective; everything else is a screen.
   — §9.346

## For the Dervish

4. **Enter the south/east edges and go straight at the palace.** Dervish units
   enter on turn 1 through any south or east edge hex (§9.322): 32 Mulazmin,
   2 Hadendowa, 6 Kehena, 5 Degheim, and 3 Dervish artillery (§9.322). Two
   routes into the city: the breachless walls must be crossed at gates or
   breaches (§6.82, §5.23) — open the wall with your artillery (§6.63) or slip
   past where the wall is absent.
   — §9.322, §6.63, §6.82

5. **Use the North Fort's guns.** The Dervish control the North Fort and may
   fire its artillery factor (§9.344) — a free turret covering the approach.
   Station the assault within its support.
   — §9.344

6. **Artillery is your gunboat-killer.** Only artillery may fire at gunboats
   (sink on CRT 3+) (§6.61). The British gunboats are a standing threat to the
   river flank (§9.321 set-up allows them in any Nile hex); your three Dervish
   artillery are the only counter.
   — §6.61

7. **Turn 1 is night — close under cover.** Turn 1 is always a night turn
   (§9.341): AE movement halved, all ranges halved (min 1), no howitzer (§8.1).
   Night favours closing to contact; use the first turn to move the mass up
   under reduced firepower.
   — §9.341, §8.1

8. **Stack by tribe for the assault.** Different Dervish tribes may not stack
   together (§5.52) and leaders stack only with their command (§5.53). The
   Mulazmin mass is the palace force; keep it coherent.
   — §5.52, §5.53

## For the Anglo-Egyptian

9. **Hold the palace; make the Dervish pay for each hex.** GORDON is the
   scenario's load-bearing point (§9.346). Garrison the approaches, screen the
   palace, and let the Dervish grind into your defensive fire — every Dervish
   unit you kill downgrades their victory level (§9.35).
   — §9.346, §9.35

10. **The walls are the first line; hold the breaches.** Units may only enter
    the walled city through a gate or breach hexside (§5.23). The Dervish
    artillery can breach on CRT 2+ (§6.63) — plug the breach and defend it with
    melee-capable stacks (§7.4).
    — §5.23, §6.63

11. **Your artillery is the wall-crusher and gunboat-clearer.** Only artillery
    fires at forts (2+) and gunboats (3+) (§6.62, §6.61). Use it to break up
    the Dervish artillery and to support the palace garrison.
    — §6.62, §6.61

12. **Gunboats hold the river flank but can't solve the palace.** Old gunboats
    set up in any Nile hexes (§9.321) and may cross White↔Blue Nile at 6
    upstream MP (§9.345). They cannot carry land units (§5.21 general rule) and
    are meat for Dervish artillery (§6.61) — use them for fire support, not
    rescue.
    — §9.321, §9.345, §5.21, §6.61

## Both sides

13. **Half-hexes are playable; watch the receding White Nile edge.** All hexes
    are playable including those showing half or less (§9.342) — the map's
    broken edge is an avenue the printed layout would not suggest.
    — §9.342

14. **Read every combat on the Dervish table.** Both players use the Dervish
    Range Effects Table for fire (§9.343) — the AE's usual table is off the
    table, so AE fire columns behave differently; verify range factors before
    committing.
    — §9.343
