# Fall of Khartoum -- Two-Player Test Game

A general gameplay walkthrough for two players exercising the UI and rules
engine. Dice outcomes are left unspecified; players should roll and resolve
each combat normally. The goal is to touch every major system at least once.

---

## Setup

### Scenario overview

The Fall of Khartoum scenario (rulebook §9.3) runs for up to **8 turns**.
Turns 1–2 are **night**; turns 3–8 are **day**. The Dervish player moves
first each turn (§9.322). The Anglo-Egyptian player controls the garrison
defending Khartoum; the Dervish player commands the assault force.

**Both players use the Dervish Range Effects Table** (§9.343) -- this is
unique to this scenario. The rulebook specifies this explicitly: all fire
attacks in FoK use the Dervish range bands regardless of which side fires.

### Victory

The game ends immediately when **GORDON is eliminated** (§9.346) -- the
instant any Dervish unit occupies the Palace hex. The victory level depends
on _which turn_ GORDON fell (§9.35):

| Gordon dies on turn | Base level       |
| ------------------- | ---------------- |
| 1–4                 | Dervish Decisive |
| 5                   | Dervish Tactical |
| 6+                  | Dervish Marginal |
| Survives to turn 8  | British Decisive |
| Survives to turn 7  | British Tactical |
| Survives to turn 6  | British Marginal |

Dervish losses shift the result toward the British end: 16–23 lost = −1
level, 24–31 = −2, 32+ = −3.

The British player wins by holding out. The Dervish player wins by
breaching the Palace quickly while keeping losses manageable.

### Deployment

**Anglo-Egyptian (17 units, §9.321):**

- GORDON is pre-placed at the **Palace** (hex 13,5). He may never move
  (§9.346).
- All other British units set up in **building or hut hexes**, at **Fort
  Makran** or **Fort Buri**, or **adjacent to any wall hexside** (meaning
  any hex that shares a side with a wall hexside, including both endpoints
  of the wall segment). Gunboats may also set up on any **Nile hex**.
- Place infantry brigades in building hexes near the wall for the −3
  defence modifier (Terrain Effects Chart). The Palace, Fort Makran, and
  Fort Buri buildings are ideal positions.
- Gunboats go on the Nile -- they provide fire support and can shuttle the
  Friendlies brigade if desired.
- Only **old gunboats** are available in this scenario (named gunboats are
  not part of the FoK order of battle).
- The Maxim guns should be positioned to cover likely breach points.

**Dervish (48 units + North Fort, §9.322):**

- The **North Fort** (hex 19,3) is pre-placed and Dervish-controlled
  (§9.344). It has artillery fire and is enclosed by its own wall ring.
- All other Dervish units enter from the **south or east map edge** (max
  row or max column).
- Group units by tribe (§5.52: different tribes may not stack together).
- The Khalifa and his Taiasha bodyguard should form the main assault group.
- Position artillery within range of the walls for potential breaching.
- Dervish gunboats go on the Nile to contest British river control.

After both sides deploy, confirm setup ready to begin.

## Turn 1 -- Night (2:00 AM)

**Night restrictions (§8.1):**

- Anglo-Egyptian movement is **halved** (round down).
- **All fire ranges are halved** for both sides (round down, range 1 stays 1).
- **Howitzer fire is prohibited** at night.
- Dervish movement is **not** halved.

### Dervish Player Turn

**Movement Phase:**

- Advance the main assault columns from the south/east map edge toward
  Khartoum. Dervish tribal units have movement 9, so they can cover ground
  quickly even across rough terrain (2 MP per hex).
- Move the three artillery pieces toward positions from which they can
  eventually target the walls (artillery range 1–7 on the Dervish table).
- Push the Khalifa and Taiasha units forward as the spearhead. They
  cannot fire (spears are melee-only) but have strong melee factors.
- Keep units of the same tribe stacked together (max 4 non-leader units
  per hex, §5.51). A Dervish leader may stack only with units of his
  command colour (§5.53).

**Defensive Fire (Direct):**

- The Anglo-Egyptian player may fire defensively. At night, rifle max range
  is 2 hexes (day range 4, halved = 2). Position defenders to fire at
  approaching Dervish at range 1–2 for doubled or normal factors.
- Roll d10, apply modifiers (terrain defence at target, Anglo-Egyptian
  direct fire +1), consult the CRT.

**Offensive Fire (Direct, then Maxim Second + Howitzer):**

- The Dervish player fires offensive direct fire. Most Dervish units are
  spear-armed (melee only), but Jehadia, Danagla, and Isa Zachneih units
  carry rifles. Position them within 2 hexes to fire at night.
- After direct fire, the Anglo-Egyptian player may fire Maxims a second
  time (§6.42). Howitzers are barred at night.

**Melee Phase:**

- If any Dervish unit is adjacent to a British unit, melee may be declared.
  At night this is unlikely on turn 1, but if the Dervish pushed aggressively
  to the walls, a melee at a gate or breach is possible.
- Melee is simultaneous: attacker and defender each roll on the CRT. The
  Dervish player gets +2, the Anglo-Egyptian gets +1 (§7.7). No terrain
  modifiers apply to melee (exception: Zariba in the historical scenario).

### Anglo-Egyptian Player Turn

- Move limited garrison units to reinforce threatened hexes (half movement
  at night for AE units).
- Fire defensively and offensively at Dervish units within range.
- The Maxim fires again in the second sub-phase if it didn't fire in
  defensive direct fire.

**End of turn:** Disrupted units recover. Advance to turn 2.

---

## Turn 2 -- Night (4:00 AM)

### Dervish Player

- Continue closing on Khartoum. By now the lead tribal units should be
  within 3–4 hexes of the outer wall.
- Move artillery into position to fire at wall hexsides (§6.63: only
  artillery may breach walls). The artillery needs LOS to the nearer
  endpoint of the wall hexside.
- Dervish gunboats may move on the Nile to engage British gunboats or
  provide fire support. Gunboat combat uses the Artillery weapon class.

**Offensive Fire -- Artillery Wall Breach (§6.63):**

- If artillery is in range and has LOS, fire at a wall hexside. Only
  artillery-class weapons may attempt this. A CRT result of **Eliminate(2)
  or higher** breaches the wall; anything else is a miss.
- A breach negates the wall for LOS, movement, melee, and ZOC. If any
  enemy units are adjacent at the instant of breaching, one is eliminated.
- Place a BREECH marker pointing at the breached hexside.

### Anglo-Egyptian Player

- Reposition to cover likely breach points. Keep infantry in building hexes
  for the −3 defence bonus.
- Fire at approaching Dervish with everything available. Night range limits
  apply.

---

## Turn 3 -- Day (6:00 AM)

Daylight! Ranges return to normal and howitzers may fire.

### Dervish Player

**Movement:**

- The assault wave should now be at or near the walls. Move units through
  any gates (gate hexsides allow passage, §5.23) or through breaches
  created by artillery.
- Stack the Khalifa with Taiasha units near the Palace approach.
- Bring the second and third assault waves forward from the east/south edge.

**Offensive Fire:**

- Now that it is day, full-range fire is available. Jehadia/Danagla rifle
  units can fire at range 1–4 (Dervish Rifles table: Normal at 1–2, Halved
  at 3–4).
- Dervish artillery fires at the walls or at garrison units behind them.
- Howitzers still cannot be used by the Dervish (they don't have any).

**Melee:**

- If Dervish units are adjacent to defenders in the walled city, declare
  melee. Remember: no melee across wall hexsides (§7.2), but melee is
  allowed through gates and breaches.
- Both sides roll on the CRT simultaneously. Dervish +2, AE +1.
- Eliminated losses are taken from meleeing units first (§7.7).
- After melee, Dervish units **must** advance into vacated hexes (§7.6:
  mandatory advance-after-melee for Dervish). They may not advance across
  wall hexsides except at gates/breaches.

### Anglo-Egyptian Player

**Howitzer Fire (§6.64):**

- Named British gunboats have howitzer capability. During the Maxim Second
  and Howitzer sub-phase, they may fire howitzers.
- Howitzer fire uses two rolls: one on the CRT, one on the impact
  scattergram. The target hex is hit on impact roll 7–10; otherwise the
  shell scatters one hex.
- Howitzers are powerful (fire factor from the gunboat) but have a minimum
  range of 4 hexes (range 1–3 = out of range on the Howitzer line).
- This is a good time to fire howitzers at Dervish concentrations in the
  open.

**Maxim Second Fire:**

- The Maxim gun may fire once in Direct Fire and once in the Second Fire
  sub-phase (§6.42) -- two shots per turn total. Position it to cover the
  most dangerous approach.

---

## Turn 4 -- Day (8:00 AM)

The critical turn. If the Dervish breach the walls and reach the Palace,
GORDON falls and the game ends with a Dervish Decisive victory.

### Dervish Player

- **Push for the Palace.** Move assault units through breaches or gates
  toward hex 13,5 (Palace). Every hex of the walled city costs 1 MP in
  Building terrain, so movement allowances are generous.
- The Khalifa stack should lead the charge. Stack the Khalifa with up to
  4 Taiasha units.
- Artillery continues to breach walls or fires at garrison units.
- Advance-after-combat: after eliminating defenders by fire, adjacent
  Dervish units may advance into the vacated hex (§6.82). Artillery may
  not advance. Units may not advance across wall hexsides except at
  gates/breaches.

**Important:** If a Dervish unit reaches the Palace hex by movement or
advance-after-combat, **GORDON is immediately eliminated** (§9.346) and
the game ends.

### Anglo-Egyptian Player

- **Defend the Palace at all costs.** Stack strong units in and around
  the Palace hex (Building terrain = −3 defence modifier).
- Use the Royal Engineers to demolish an adjacent Dervish fort if one is
  threatening (§6.53: Engineers spend a turn adjacent, no offensive
  fire/melee; target destroyed at end of turn if Engineers remain adjacent
  and undisrupted).
- Fire everything at the lead Dervish columns. Concentrate fire to
  eliminate or disrupt key units.
- A disrupted unit may not move, fire, or melee (§5) -- disrupting the
  Khalifa's bodyguard buys critical time.

---

## Turns 5–6 -- Day (10:00 AM – Noon)

The battle reaches its climax. The Dervish player is running out of time
-- GORDON dying on turn 5 gives only Dervish Tactical, and turn 6+ gives
Dervish Marginal (before loss penalties).

### Dervish Player

- If the walls are breached, funnel everything toward the Palace.
- If the walls are still intact, keep bombarding with artillery. Consider
  a feint through a gate while the main assault comes through a breach.
- Dervish gunboats on the Nile may fire at British gunboats (§6.61:
  only artillery may fire at gunboats -- Dervish gunboats qualify).

### Anglo-Egyptian Player

- Delay and attrit. Every turn GORDON survives improves the British
  victory level.
- Use defensive fire every turn to thin the assault waves.
- Reposition gunboats to maintain fire support. Gunboats are immune to
  melee (§7.1) and may only be damaged by artillery fire (§6.61).
- If a gunboat is hit by a river mine (optional rule §10.12), roll for
  damage: 1–4 no effect, 5–7 engines lost (drifts with current), 8–10 sunk.

---

## Turns 7–8 -- Day (2:00 – 4:00 PM)

If GORDON has survived this long, the British player is in a strong
position. Turn 8 is the last turn.

### Dervish Player

- Desperate final assault. Even a marginal Dervish victory requires
  reaching the Palace on turn 6+.
- Check your losses: if 24+ Dervish units have been eliminated, you lose
  2 victory levels even if you take the Palace.

### Anglo-Egyptian Player

- Hunker down and defend. Fire defensively at anything in range.
- Avoid advancing into Dervish ZOC unnecessarily -- a lost unit is 3 VP
  to the Dervish (§9.14: Anglo-Egyptian land unit eliminated).

## Key Rules Reference for This Game

### Turn Sequence (§4)

Each player-turn proceeds:

1. **Movement Phase** -- move units up to their allowance
2. **Defensive Fire** -- Direct Fire sub-phase, then (AE only) Maxim Second
   Fire + Howitzer sub-phase
3. **Offensive Fire** -- Direct Fire sub-phase, then (AE only) Maxim Second
   Fire + Howitzer sub-phase
4. **Melee Phase** -- declare and resolve melee attacks
5. **End of Turn** -- recover disrupted units, advance turn

### Stacking (§5.51–5.53)

- Max 4 units per hex (leaders and gunboats don't count toward the limit).
- Different Dervish tribes may not stack together.
- A Dervish leader may stack only with units of his command colour.

### Fire Combat (§6)

1. Select firing unit(s) and target hex with enemy units.
2. Determine range and consult the **Dervish Range Effects Table** (both
   sides use it in this scenario, §9.343).
3. Compute effective fire factor: printed factor × range band modifier.
4. Sum all firing units' effective factors to get the CRT row.
5. Roll d10 and apply modifiers:
   - Anglo-Egyptian direct fire: +1 (§6.24)
   - Terrain defence modifier at target hex
   - Zariba thorn hedge: −2 (if applicable)
   - Zariba trench (entrenched): −4 (if applicable)
6. Look up the result on the CRT: No Effect, Disrupt, or Eliminate 1–5.
7. Apply results: disrupted units are turned face-up and may not act until
   recovered; eliminated units are removed.

### Melee Combat (§7)

1. Both sides must be in adjacent hexes (no wall or thorn-hedge blocking,
   §7.2).
2. Sum melee factors of all attacking/defending units.
3.    Both attacker and defender roll d10 simultaneously.
4. Apply melee modifiers: Dervish +2, Anglo-Egyptian +1 (§7.7).
5. Look up each roll on the CRT. Losses are applied to the _other_ side's
   result -- attacker losses come from attacker's roll result, defender
   losses from defender's roll result.
6. Melee losses must be taken from meleeing units first (§7.7).
7. Cavalry/camel units may retreat 2 hexes before melee resolves (§7.5).
8. After melee, Dervish units **must** advance into vacated enemy hexes (§7.6).

### Night Rules (§8.1)

- Anglo-Egyptian movement halved (round down).
- All fire ranges halved for both sides (round down, range 1 stays 1).
- Howitzer fire prohibited.
- Desertion roll for Dervish on the first night turn (campaign game only;
  not applicable in Fall of Khartoum).

### Zones of Control (§5.41–5.44)

- Every non-disrupted unit exerts ZOC into all 6 adjacent hexes.
- Anglo-Egyptian leaders exert no ZOC.
- Gunboats exert ZOC only against enemy gunboats.
- ZOC does not cross wall, khor, or Zariba hexsides.
- ZOC does not extend into or out of Nile hexes (except gunboats).
- A unit entering an enemy ZOC must stop there (§5.26, §5.43).

### Wall Breaching (§6.63)

- Only artillery may fire to breach a wall hexside.
- Requires a CRT result of Eliminate(2) or higher.
- A breach negates the wall for all purposes. If enemy units are adjacent,
  one is eliminated.
- Place a BREECH marker.

### Royal Engineers Demolition (§6.53)

- Engineers move adjacent to a fort or wall hexside and end movement.
- They may not fire offensively or melee that turn.
- At end of turn, if still adjacent and undisrupted, the target is
  destroyed (fort removed or wall breached).

## Exercises to Run During This Game

These are specific system-exercises to ensure full UI/logic coverage:

1. **Stacking validation** -- try to stack 5 units in one hex (should
   fail), try to mix two Dervish tribes (should fail), try to stack a
   Dervish leader with a non-command tribe (should fail).
2. **Night movement** -- move an AE unit at night and verify halved
   allowance. Move a Dervish unit and verify full allowance.
3. **Night fire** -- fire an AE rifle at range 3 at night (should fail:
   night max range is 2). Fire at range 2 (should succeed at Normal band).
4. **Howitzer at night** -- attempt howitzer fire during a night turn
   (should fail, §8.1).
5. **Wall breach** -- fire artillery at a wall hexside. Resolve the CRT
   roll. If ≥ Eliminate(2), place the breach marker and eliminate one
   adjacent defender.
6. **Gate passage** -- move a unit through a gate hexside (should succeed,
   gates allow passage). Move a unit through a wall hexside (should fail).
7. **GORDON check** -- move a Dervish unit to the Palace hex and verify
   that GORDON is eliminated and the game ends.
8. **Advance after fire** -- eliminate a defender by offensive fire, then
   advance an adjacent attacker into the vacated hex.
9. **Melee** -- declare melee between adjacent hexes. Verify the defender
   retreat window for cavalry/camel units. Resolve melee and apply
   simultaneous results.
10. **Disruption recovery** -- at end of a player's turn, verify that
    their disrupted units recover.
11. **Gunboat combat** -- fire a gunboat at an enemy gunboat (artillery
    weapon class, §6.61). Verify that only artillery may target gunboats.
12. **Friendlies transport** (if Isa Zachneih is eliminated) -- load a
    Friendlies unit onto a gunboat, cross the Nile, disembark on the far
    bank (three-turn sequence, §5.21).
13. **Dervish gunboat Nile crossing** -- move a gunboat between White and
    Blue Nile mouths off-board (§9.345: flat 6 upstream MP).
14. **Victory level check** -- at game end, verify that the correct
    FoKVictoryLevel is computed from Gordon's death turn and Dervish losses.
