### Play aids and components
Terrain Effects Chart, Combat Tables (Range Effects Tables + Combat Results Table), Line of Sight Table with its Special LOS Notes, Howitzer Fire Scattergram, Turn Record Track, Turn Sequence chart, Campaign Game Order of Appearance card, one ten-sided die, the counter sheet, a Game Turn marker, BREACH markers, blank counters (marking Zariba construction), the rules booklet and the box. (§2.1, 2.2, 2.4, 6.53, 6.63, 5.3)

### Time
- **Game Turn:** two Player Turns. A day turn is two hours.
- **Player Turn:** Movement, then Fire Combat (defensive fire, then offensive fire), then Melee.
- **Phases and subphases:** Movement Phase, Fire Combat Phase (Defensive Fire, Offensive Fire), Direct Fire Subphase, Maxim Second Fire and Howitzer Fire Subphase, Melee Phase.
- **Night game turn:** turns 9, 10, 19 and 20 of the campaign game. Turn 1 of Fall of Khartoum is also a night turn.
- **Scenario length:** campaign 22 turns, historical 4 turns, Fall of Khartoum variable (rarely past 5).

### Victory concepts
Victory points (VP), "superiority" (the VP difference), victory levels (Decisive, Strategic [historical only], Tactical, Marginal, Draw), net victory (historical), level loss from casualties (Fall of Khartoum), and alternative sudden-death decisive conditions.

## A.3 Mechanisms (rule systems)

1. **IGO-UGO turn structure with interleaved fire:** the non-moving player's defensive fire comes before the mover's offensive fire (§4, 6.4).
2. **Asymmetric fire subphases:** only the Anglo-Egyptian side gets the Maxim second fire and the howitzer subphase, and gets it on defence as well as offence (§4, 6.42).
3. **Movement points and terrain costs** (Terrain Effects Chart). No saving MP between turns or passing them to other units (§5.1).
4. **Directional river movement** for gunboats, using two allowances (§5.24).
5. **Restricted terrain:** Nile, walled city, gates and breaches, the Zariba's end hexsides (§5.22, 5.23, 9.233).
6. **Zones of control:** sticky ("stop on entry"), no MP cost, plus a long table of directional exceptions (§5.4).
7. **Stacking:** 4 units per hex; leaders free; gunboats alone; no mixing tribes; Dervish leaders stack only with their own colour; checked at the end of movement and during combat (§5.5).
8. **Brigade integrity bonus** (§5.54, 6.24).
9. **Fire combat pipeline:** LOS check → range multiplier (×3, ×2, ×1, ×½, out of range) → sum the factors → terrain modifier → Anglo-Egyptian bonuses → d10 clamped to 1..10 → CRT (§6.2, CRT notes).
10. **Halving arithmetic:** round down per unit, minimum 1 (§6.16).
11. **Fire once / be fired at once** per fire phase, with exceptions (§6.14, 6.42).
12. **Target-class restrictions:** only artillery hits gunboats (3+), forts (2+) and walls (2+) (§6.61–6.63).
13. **Indirect fire with scatter:** howitzer + Scattergram (§6.64).
14. **Engineering:** Zariba construction and Royal Engineer demolition take a whole player turn, the unit must stay adjacent, and it gives up its offensive actions (§5.3, 6.53).
15. **Simultaneous melee:** both sides roll the CRT with side modifiers and no terrain modifiers except the Zariba (§7).
16. **Advance after combat:** eligibility rules; forced for Dervish after melee (§6.82, 7.6).
17. **Disruption state:** no ZOC, no movement, no fire, no melee; recovers at the end of the owner's turn (CRT notes).
18. **Night effects:** Anglo-Egyptian movement halved, no howitzers, ranges halved (§8.1).
19. **One-time desertion event** (§8.2).
20. **Reinforcement schedules** with entry areas and per-turn limits (§9.112, 9.113).
21. **Victory point accounting** with a superiority table and sudden-death decisives (§9.14). Net victory levels (§9.24). Turn-of-capture victory with casualty demotion (§9.35).
22. **Hidden information optional rules:** river mines and the river chain (§10).

## A.4 Numbers at a glance
- Hex ≈ 400–440 yd; day turn = 2 h; unit = 400–700 men.
- Stacking limit 4 (leaders and gunboats excepted).
- Anglo-Egyptian direct fire +1; brigade integrity +1; Dervish melee +2; Anglo-Egyptian melee +1; Friendlies melee +2 (they use the Dervish modifier).
- Fort defence −3. Thorn hedge −2 to Dervish fire. Trench −4 to Dervish fire vs entrenched units. Dervish melee vs entrenched: −2 instead of +2. Crossing a Zariba end hexside: +2 MP.
- CRT result needed: 3+ sinks a gunboat or the chain; 2+ kills a fort or breaches a wall.
- Howitzer: 5 named gunboats, range 4–10, target hex hit on a scatter roll of 7–10.
- Die is a d10. Modified rolls are clamped to 1..10. D = disrupt ½ of the target hex's units, rounded up.
- Desertion = 1½ × d10 units.
- Campaign: 17 forts, 22 turns, night turns 9/10/19/20, Anglo-Egyptian entry limit 3 gunboats + 12 land units per turn on turns 2–3, all leaders on by turn 4.
- Tomb 25 VP; British leader / gunboat 10 VP each; Khalifa 10 VP.
- Historical: Zariba of 13 hexes, 4 turns, Dervish units within 3 hexes of their leader.
- Fall of Khartoum: Gordon decisive thresholds at turns 4 / 5 / 6 / 6 / 7 / 8; casualty demotion bands 16–23 / 24–31 / 32+.

# Part B — Rules Paragraph by Paragraph, with Player Questions

Each entry gives a short summary of the passage, the concepts it introduces, and questions a
player in the middle of a game might ask that the passage answers.

## §1 Introduction

### §1.1 ¶1 — What the game is
Simulates Omdurman on 2 Sept 1898. One player is Kitchener (Anglo-Egyptian), the other the Khalifa (Dervish). Multi-player is possible, by tribe or brigade.
- *Concepts:* the two sides, multi-player command split.
- *Questions:*
  - "Which side am I if I'm 'the Khalifa'?"
  - "Can four of us play? How do we split the armies?"
  - "If I'm commanding the Hadendowa only, whose units can I touch?" (By tribe or brigade.)
  - "What historical day is this battle?"

### §1.1 ¶2 — Recommended learning order
- *Questions:*
  - "We're new. Which scenario do we start with?" (Fall of Khartoum, then the historical scenario, then the campaign.)
  - "Is this a beginner's game?"

### §1.1 ¶3 — Spelling
- *Questions:*
  - "The counter says 'Taiasha' but my book says 'Ta'aisha'. Same thing?"
  - "Why don't the tribe names match Wikipedia?"

### §1.2 — Game scale
- *Concepts:* hex scale, turn length, unit size, one counter per gunboat, leader counters (Dervish leaders include their retinues).
- *Questions:*
  - "How far is one hex in yards?"
  - "How much real time passes each turn?"
  - "How many men does this battalion counter represent?"
  - "Is each gunboat counter a single boat or a flotilla?"
  - "Why can a Dervish leader fight but Kitchener can't?" (Dervish leader counters include their retinues.)

## §2 Game Components

### §2.1 ¶1 — The Omdurman map
Charts are printed on the map. Lettered hexes and Zariba hexsides apply only in the historical scenario. In the campaign game the Zariba hexsides are clear terrain unless constructed. Every full hex is playable, including the 7 scattergram hexes.
- *Questions:*
  - "There's a big 'K' on this hex. Does it matter in the campaign game?" (No.)
  - "Do the Zariba trenches count in the campaign game?" (No, they're clear terrain unless the Anglo-Egyptian player builds them under 5.3.)
  - "My unit wants to move onto the scattergram drawing. Is that a legal hex?" (Yes.)
  - "Where's the Terrain Effects Chart?" (Top of the map.)
  - "Where's the CRT?" (Lower right.)

### §2.1 ¶2 — The Khartoum mini-map
- *Questions:*
  - "Why is there a gap in Khartoum's wall?" (The Nile washed it away.)
  - "How do the two maps line up?" (Khartoum's north edge joins the middle of Omdurman's south edge.)
  - "Is Khartoum in play during the campaign game?" (It lay in ruins in 1898; the campaign uses the Omdurman map.)

### §2.2 — Play aids
- *Questions:*
  - "Which table tells me if terrain slows me down?" (Terrain Effects Chart.)
  - "Which table tells me if I can see the target?" (LOS Table, back of the rulebook.)
  - "Where are range effects listed?" (Combat Tables.)
  - "Can I fire at a unit I can't see?" (No: LOS blocks direct fire.)

### §2.31 — Dervish weapons
Artillery, gunboats and forts use the artillery line. Jehadia, Danagla and Isa Zachneih use rifles. Everyone else, leaders included, has spears and swords.
- *Questions:*
  - "Which range line does my fort use?"
  - "Can my Baggara fire at all?" (Yes, with their printed fire factor, as spears and swords (§2.31); their strength is melee.)
  - "Is Isa Zachneih a rifle unit?" (Yes.)
  - "Can Osman Digna shoot?" (Leaders are spears and swords. His fire factor applies at whatever range that weapon allows.)

### §2.32 — Anglo-Egyptian weapons
Rifles are the default. Maxims use the Maxim line. Artillery and old gunboats use the Artillery line. Named gunboats use the Howitzer, Artillery and Maxim lines. Friendlies follow 6.52.
- *Questions:*
  - "Which line does the *Sultan* use?" (Any of the three.)
  - "Does an old gunboat have Maxims?" (No: Artillery line only.)
  - "Do the Friendlies use our rifle line?" (No: the Dervish table.)
  - "What range line does the Camel Corps use?" (Rifles.)

### §2.3 — Sample counters
- *Questions:*
  - "Which number on the counter is melee?" (The middle one.)
  - "What does '10/16' on a gunboat mean?" (Upstream / downstream movement.)
  - "What's the '6×2' on the *Sultan*?" (Maxims that fire twice.)
  - "Where's the brigade ID on my infantry?" (Upper right, e.g. 4th Egyptian.)
  - "Kitchener is 0·0·15. So he has no fire or melee at all?"

### §2.4 — Inventory
- *Questions:*
  - "What kind of die do we use?" (One d10.)
  - "Where's the reinforcement schedule?" (The Order of Appearance card.)

## §3 Getting Started
Unfold the map. Dervish sits west, Anglo-Egyptian east. Read the rules, pick a scenario, punch out only the counters you need.
- *Questions:*
  - "Which side of the table do I sit on as the Dervish?" (West.)
  - "Do I need to punch every counter to play Fall of Khartoum?"
  - "Which direction is my 'home' edge?"

## §4 Turn Sequence

### §4 ¶1 — Game Turns and Player Turns
- *Questions:*
  - "How many player turns make a game turn?" (Two.)
  - "Who goes first?" (Depends on the scenario. Anglo-Egyptian in the campaign; Dervish in the historical and Fall of Khartoum.)

### §4 A — Anglo-Egyptian Player Turn
Movement → Fire Combat (a. Dervish defensive fire; b. Anglo-Egyptian offensive fire: 1. direct, 2. Maxim second fire + howitzer) → Anglo-Egyptian melee.
- *Questions:*
  - "I just moved next to his spearmen. Who shoots first?" (The Dervish fire defensively first.)
  - "When do my howitzers fire on my own turn?" (After all direct fire.)
  - "Do I melee before or after shooting?" (After.)
  - "Does the Dervish player get to melee during my turn?" (No.)

### §4 B — Dervish Player Turn
Movement → Fire Combat (a. Anglo-Egyptian defensive fire: direct, then Maxim second + howitzer; b. Dervish offensive fire) → Dervish melee.
- *Questions:*
  - "Do my Maxims get two shots when he's attacking me?" (Yes, defensive fire has both subphases.)
  - "Can my gunboats use howitzers during his turn?" (Yes, in defensive fire, day turns only.)
  - "Can the Dervish shoot twice with anything?" (No. No second subphase for the Dervish.)
  - "If my defensive fire disrupts his units, can they still melee me?" (No: disrupted units can't melee.)

### §4 C — End of turn
- *Questions:*
  - "When does the turn marker move?" (After both player turns.)
  - "How do we know when the game ends?" (End of the scenario's length.)

## §5 Movement

### §5.11 — Movement allowance
- *Questions:*
  - "How far can this cavalry move?" (Up to its printed 15, minus terrain costs.)
  - "What does it cost to move into a hut hex?" (See the TEC.)

### §5.12 — Move any or all
- *Questions:*
  - "Do I have to move every unit?" (No.)
  - "Can I just leave the artillery where it is?" (Yes.)
  - "What stops me moving further?" (Movement allowance, terrain, enemy ZOC.)

### §5.13 — No saving or transferring MP
- *Questions:*
  - "My unit sat still last turn. Can it move double this turn?" (No.)
  - "Can my leader give his leftover MP to the infantry he's with?" (No.)
  - "I have 2 MP left and the next hex costs 3. Do I keep the 2?" (No, they're lost.)

### §5.21 — River transport (Friendlies exception)
Gunboats carry no land units, except that the Friendlies may be ferried east → west once Isa Zachneih is eliminated: (a) start the turn adjacent, then load; (b) next turn the gunboat moves to a Nile hex adjacent to the west bank; (c) the third turn, disembark and move normally.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my gunboat carry the Lancers across the river?" (No.)
  - "When can the Friendlies cross?" (Only after Isa Zachneih is gone.)
  - "How many turns does the ferry take?" (Three Anglo-Egyptian turns.)
  - "Can the Friendlies move on the turn they get off the boat?" (Yes, normally, paying the first hex's terrain cost.)
  - "Does the gunboat have to stay put while unloading?" (No, it can also move normally that turn.)
  - "Is the loaded gunboat breaking the 'gunboats stack alone' rule?" (This is the stated exception.)
  - "Why would I bother?" (Friendlies lost on the west bank cost 3 VP instead of 1, but the Friendlies add west-bank strength.)

### §5.22 — Land units and the Nile
- *Questions:*
  - "Can infantry wade onto an island hex across the Nile?" (Not into Nile River hexes.)
  - "Can anything besides gunboats go into the river?"

### §5.23 — The walled city of Omdurman
Dervish: only the Khalifa, the 3 artillery units and the Taiasha. Anglo-Egyptian: anyone except gunboats and Friendlies. Entry and exit only through a gate or breach hexside.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my Baggara fall back into the walled city?" (No.)
  - "Can the Khalifa leave the city?" (Yes, through a gate or breach.)
  - "Can my Highlanders climb the wall?" (No. They need a gate or a breach.)
  - "Can the Friendlies help storm the city?" (No.)
  - "Which Dervish units can garrison the inner city?"

### §5.24 — Gunboat upstream/downstream movement
The smaller number is upstream, the larger downstream. Directions can be mixed, but one upstream hex caps the whole turn at the upstream allowance.
- *Questions:*
  - "Which way is upstream?" (Against the arrows in the Nile.)
  - "My gunboat went 10 hexes downstream. Can it come back 2 upstream?" (No: moving upstream at all caps the whole turn at the upstream 10, and 10 + 2 = 12.)
  - "If I move one hex upstream, can I still use the 16?" (No. The 10 becomes the cap.)
  - "Can I go 15 downstream and then stop?" (Yes.)

### §5.25 — Forts never move
- *Questions:*
  - "Can I reposition my fort?" (Never, once placed.)

### §5.26 — Stop in an enemy ZOC
- *Questions:*
  - "I moved next to an enemy unit. Can I keep going?" (No, stop immediately.)

### §5.3 — Constructing the Zariba
Campaign game only. Built only in the printed positions. Any Anglo-Egyptian infantry unit that begins and ends its player turn adjacent to (and on the Nile side of) Zariba hexsides builds every hexside it is adjacent to. It may not fire offensively or melee that turn. Mark it with a blank counter.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can I build a zariba anywhere I like?" (Only where it's printed.)
  - "Which units can build it?" (Anglo-Egyptian infantry only: not cavalry, artillery or Maxims.)
  - "How long does it take?" (One full Anglo-Egyptian player turn: begin and end adjacent.)
  - "Can my builders still shoot back on the Dervish turn?" (The ban is on offensive fire and melee. Defensive fire isn't mentioned.)
  - "Does one battalion build the whole Zariba?" (Only the hexsides it's adjacent to.)
  - "Which side do I have to be on?" (The Nile side.)
  - "How do we remember who's building?" (Blank counter.)

### §5.41 — Who has a ZOC
Every unit except Anglo-Egyptian leaders projects a ZOC into its 6 adjacent hexes. Gunboats exert one only against gunboats. Disrupted units have none.
- *Questions:*
  - "Does Kitchener alone pin enemy spearmen?" (No ZOC.)
  - "Does a gunboat stop infantry walking along the bank?" (No, only enemy gunboats.)
  - "My battalion got disrupted. Can the enemy walk past it?" (Yes, it has no ZOC.)
  - "Do Dervish leaders have a ZOC?" (Yes.)
  - "Do forts?" (Yes, even unoccupied: 5.44 and 6.54.)

### §5.42 — ZOC costs no MP
- *Questions:*
  - "Does entering or leaving a ZOC cost extra?" (No.)

### §5.43 — Stop; next turn withdraw or move ZOC to ZOC
- *Questions:*
  - "I'm stuck next to the enemy. Can I leave next turn?" (Yes, withdraw.)
  - "Can I slide from one enemy's ZOC directly into another's?" (Yes, in the next movement phase, then stop again.)
  - "Can I move through two ZOC hexes in one turn?" (No, you stop at the first.)

### §5.44 — ZOC propagation exceptions
| Boundary | Out of | Into |
|---|---|---|

### §5.51 — Stacking limit
Four units per hex. Leaders are free. Gunboats stack alone (except 5.21). Moving through friendly units costs nothing extra. The limit applies at the end of movement and during combat.
- *Questions:*
  - "How many battalions can I put in a hex?" (4.)
  - "Does Kitchener count toward the 4?" (No.)
  - "Can two gunboats share a hex?" (No.)
  - "Can my column pass through an already-full hex?" (Yes, the limit only applies at the end of movement and during combat.)
  - "Can an advance after combat overstack?" (No, the limit applies during combat. See 7.6 "up to the stacking limit.")

### §5.52 — Tribes don't mix
- *Questions:*
  - "Can Mulazmin and Jehadia stack? They're both green." (No.)
  - "Can I stack Baggara with Jaalin?" (No.)

### §5.53 — Dervish leader stacking
Leaders don't have to stack. If they do, only with units of their command (colour).
- *Questions:*
  - "Can Osman Digna join a stack of Baggara?" (No, only Hadendowa.)
  - "Can Sheik El Din stand alone?" (Yes.)
  - "Can Sheik El Din stack with Jehadia?" (Yes, his colour.)

### §5.54 — Brigade integrity
All four battalions of one Anglo-Egyptian infantry brigade in one hex during a combat phase get +1 to fire, provided all fire at the same hex. This adds to the normal +1.
- *Questions:*
  - "What does '3E' on my counter mean?" (3rd Egyptian Brigade.)
  - "Do I get a bonus for keeping the brigade together?" (+1 to fire.)
  - "If one battalion was eliminated, do the three survivors still get it?" (No, it needs all four.)
  - "If I split fire between two hexes, do I lose it?" (Yes.)
  - "Can I mix battalions from two brigades to get the bonus?" (No, they must be the same brigade.)
  - "What's my total die modifier with integrity?" (+2.)
  - "Does it apply on defensive fire?" ("In any combat phase": yes.)

## §6 Fire Combat

### §6.11 — Fire factor
- *Questions:*
  - "Which number is my shooting strength?" (The first one, the fire combat factor.)

### §6.12 — Fire is voluntary
- *Questions:*
  - "Do I have to shoot at the unit next to me?" (No.)
  - "Can I hold fire to stay hidden?" (Fire is never required.)

### §6.13 — Fire factors are unitary
- *Questions:*
  - "Can I split my 10-factor battery between two hexes?" (No, one unit fires at one hex.)

### §6.14 — Combining fire; fire once, be fired at once
- *Questions:*
  - "Can three battalions pool their fire on one target?" (Yes, one combined attack.)
  - "Can units from different hexes combine?" (Yes, all eligible units.)
  - "That Baggara stack was already shot this phase. Can my artillery hit it again?" (No, except in the Maxim/howitzer subphase per 6.42.)
  - "Can a unit fire twice?" (Only Maxims and gunboats.)

### §6.15 — Dividing a stack
- *Questions:*
  - "My stack has 4 units. Can they hit 4 different hexes?" (Yes, but a brigade loses its integrity bonus.)

### §6.16 — Halving arithmetic
Round down per unit; never below 1.
- *Questions:*
  - "Four 9-factor battalions at half strength. Is that 18 or 16?" (16: 4 each.)
  - "My 1-factor unit is halved. Is that 0?" (No, never below 1.)
  - "Do I halve the total or each unit?" (Each unit.)

### §6.21 — Check LOS first
- *Questions:*
  - "Before rolling, what do I check first?" (LOS, except for howitzers.)

### §6.22 — Range multiplier
×3, ×2, ×1, ×½, or out of range, then sum.
- *Questions:*
  - "Is my rifle doubled at range 1?" (See the Range Effects Table. At night, 8.1 gives the example of doubled at range 1.)
  - "Is the target out of range for spears?"
  - "Do units at different ranges get different multipliers before adding?" (Yes, each is multiplied, then summed.)

### §6.23 — Terrain modifier and CRT lookup
- *Questions:*
  - "The target is in huts. What do I subtract?" (Per the TEC.)
  - "How do I read the CRT?" (Factors firing × net die roll.)
  - "Is the terrain bonus a column shift or a die modifier?" (A die modifier.)

### §6.24 — Anglo-Egyptian accuracy bonus
+1 on all Anglo-Egyptian direct fire, +1 more for brigade integrity. They add together.
- *Questions:*
  - "Do the British always get +1 when shooting?" (On direct fire.)
  - "Does the bonus stack with integrity?" (Yes.)
  - "Do the Dervish get any fire bonus?" (No.)
  - "Does the +1 apply to howitzer fire?" (It says *direct* fire. Howitzer fire is indirect. See Part C.)

### §6.3 — Line of Sight Table
Cross-reference the firer's terrain with the target's. Footnotes and Special LOS Notes apply. Without LOS you can't fire, except with howitzers.
- *Questions:*
  - "I'm in a khor and he's on a ridge. Can I see him?"
  - "Does the Mahdi's Tomb block sight?" (See the Special LOS Notes.)
  - "Can my gunboats shell troops behind a hill?" (Only with howitzer fire.)

### §6.4 — Fire sequence per player turn
On the Dervish turn: Anglo-Egyptian 6.41 + 6.42 defensively, then Dervish 6.41. On the Anglo-Egyptian turn: Dervish 6.41 defensively, then Anglo-Egyptian 6.41 + 6.42.
- *Questions:*
  - "Who resolves fire first on my turn?" (The defender.)
  - "Does the Dervish ever use the second subphase?" (No.)

### §6.41 — Direct Fire Subphase
Allocate all fire first, then resolve in any order.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can I see the result of my first attack before committing the next unit?" (No, allocate everything first.)
  - "Can I choose which attack to resolve first?" (Yes.)
  - "If my first attack wipes out the target, can I redirect the second?" (No, it was already allocated.)

### §6.42 — Maxim Second Fire and Howitzer Subphase
Named gunboats fire howitzers; all Maxims fire again. Allocate, then resolve. Howitzers combine with Maxims only if the howitzer impacts the intended hex. A Maxim that skipped 6.41 still fires only once here. Targets already fired at in 6.41 may be fired at again.
- *Questions:*
  - "My Maxims didn't fire in direct fire. Do they get two shots now?" (No, only one.)
  - "Can I hit the stack my rifles just shot?" (Yes.)
  - "Can I add a howitzer to my Maxim attack?" (Only if it lands on the intended hex.)
  - "If the howitzer scatters, does the Maxim attack still happen?" (The manual does not say. See Part C.)
  - "Can old gunboats fire in this subphase?" (No, named gunboats only. Maxims are on the named boats and the Maxim Battery.)

### §6.51 — Leaders
Dervish leaders have fire, melee and movement factors; they attack, melee and die like combat units, and stack free. Anglo-Egyptian leaders have movement only. They die if (a) alone in a hex a Dervish unit enters or passes through, or (b) all combat units stacked with them are eliminated. At least one must survive to take the Tomb.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can the Khalifa lead a melee charge?" (Yes.)
  - "Can my spearmen kill Hunter by running over him?" (Yes, if he's alone.)
  - "If the stack Kitchener is in gets wiped out, does he escape?" (No, he dies.)
  - "Can Kitchener be shot on his own?" (The rule names only two ways he dies: overrun when alone, or his stack being eliminated. See Part C.)
  - "Why do I need a leader alive?" (To take the Tomb.)
  - "Does a leader pass-through stop my Dervish unit?" (Anglo-Egyptian leaders have no ZOC, so no.)

### §6.52 — Friendlies
Dervish range table, Dervish melee modifier, no walled city, may cross to the west bank.
- *Questions:*
  - "Do the Friendlies get the British +1 accuracy?" (They fire on the Dervish table. See Part C.)
  - "What melee modifier do the Friendlies use?" (+2, the Dervish one.)
  - "Can they enter Omdurman's walled city?" (No.)

### §6.53 — Royal Engineers demolition
Move adjacent to a fort or wall hexside and end movement there. No offensive fire or melee that combat phase. If still adjacent and undisrupted at the end of the Anglo-Egyptian player turn, the target is destroyed: remove the fort or place a breach marker. Effects on adjacent enemies follow 6.62 and 6.63. The Engineers may be stacked with others.
- *Questions:*
  - "How do I blow a hole in the wall without artillery?" (Royal Engineers.)
  - "How long does demolition take?" (Move adjacent, then it succeeds at the end of that same player turn if they survive undisrupted.)
  - "If the Dervish defensive fire disrupts my Engineers, does the demolition fail?" (Yes.)
  - "Can my Engineers shoot while demolishing?" (No offensive fire or melee.)
  - "Do defenders next to a breached wall die?" (One adjacent enemy unit is eliminated, per 6.63.)
  - "Does the fort's garrison suffer when the Engineers destroy it?" (One unit is eliminated, per 6.62.)
  - "Can I protect the Engineers by stacking them with infantry?" (Yes.)

### §6.54 — Forts
A fort's artillery fires even when unstacked. Its melee value is defence-only. −3 to enemy fire on friendly units inside. No one may occupy an enemy fort or advance into an empty one. Friendly forts cost nothing extra to enter or leave. Forts are destroyed by artillery fire, infantry melee or the Engineers. They have a ZOC when empty.
- *Questions:*
  - "Does an empty fort still shoot?" (Yes.)
  - "Can my fort charge out and melee?" (No.)
  - "Can I garrison a fort?" (Yes, a friendly one, at no extra cost.)
  - "My infantry cleared out the defenders. Can they move into the fort?" (No, never into an enemy fort.)
  - "How much does a fort protect its garrison?" (−3 to the enemy fire roll.)
  - "Can my rifles hit the fort itself?" (No, only artillery (6.62). Infantry can melee it.)
  - "Do forts give VP?" (Zero to the Anglo-Egyptian player, 9.14.)

### §6.61 — Firing at gunboats
Artillery only; a result of 3+ sinks; anything else misses.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my Jehadia riflemen shoot at a gunboat?" (No.)
  - "Does a D result disrupt a gunboat?" (No: anything under 3 is a miss.)
  - "Can my forts shoot at gunboats?" (Implied yes: forts fire an artillery factor, but 6.61 says only "artillery". See Part C.)

### §6.62 — Firing at forts
Artillery only; 2+ eliminates; on destruction one unit inside is eliminated too.
- *Questions:*
  - "What do I need on the CRT to flatten a fort?" (2+.)
  - "Does destroying the fort kill the whole garrison?" (One unit.)
  - "Is firing at the fort a separate attack from firing at the units in it?" (Only artillery can target a fort. See Part C.)

### §6.63 — Breaching walls
Artillery only; 2+ breaches. The wall is negated for LOS. Place a BREACH marker with its arrow on the hexside. One adjacent enemy unit is eliminated.
- *Questions:*
  - "How do I open the wall?" (Artillery 2+ or the Engineers.)
  - "After a breach, can I see into the city?" (Yes, LOS through that hexside.)
  - "Does the breach let me enter?" (Yes. Movement, ZOC, melee and advance all treat breaches as passable.)
  - "Can Khartoum's walls be breached too?" (Yes.)

### §6.64 — Howitzer fire
The five named gunboats fire their artillery factor as direct fire in 6.41, and the same factor again as howitzer fire in 6.42. Never at night. Pick a target 4–10 hexes away, ignoring LOS. Roll twice: the first roll is the CRT, the second the scatter. 7–10 hits the target. Results always apply, even on friendly units.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my gunboat fire its big guns twice a turn?" (Yes, direct, then howitzer.)
  - "How close can I drop a howitzer shell?" (Minimum 4 hexes.)
  - "Can I shell the Mahdi's Tomb hex without seeing it?" (Yes, LOS is ignored.)
  - "What happens if I roll a 3 on the second die?" (It scatters per the Scattergram.)
  - "If it scatters onto my own battalion, does it count?" (Yes.)
  - "Howitzers at night?" (No.)
  - "Which die is which?" (First CRT, second impact.)

### §6.7 — Defensive fire
All the non-moving player's units may fire at any of the mover's units in range. No advance after defensive fire.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can I shoot at units that didn't move this turn?" (Yes, any of the moving player's units in range.)
  - "His unit fled after my defensive fire. Can I move into the empty hex?" (No.)

### §6.81 — Offensive fire
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my artillery fire at maximum range during my turn?" (Yes.)

### §6.82 — Advance after offensive fire
A vacated enemy hex may be entered by units that took part in the attack and were adjacent. Artillery can't advance. No advancing across a wall except at a gate or breach. Never across a khor.
- *Questions:*
  - "I eliminated that stack. Can I move in?" (Yes, if you were adjacent and took part.)
  - "Can my supporting artillery follow up?" (No.)
  - "Can a unit two hexes away that fired advance?" (No, it must be adjacent.)
  - "Can I advance across the khor?" (Never.)
  - "Does advancing count as movement?" (It happens in the combat phase. Gordon's rule says outright that an advance into the palace kills him. For other Anglo-Egyptian leaders see Part C.)

## §7 Melee

### §7.1 — Melee strength; gunboats excluded

### §7.2 — Adjacency; walls

### §7.3 — Simultaneous
- *Questions:*
  - "My battalion was destroyed in the melee. Does it still hurt the attacker?" (Yes, it still rolls.)
  - "Who rolls first?" (It doesn't matter, the results are simultaneous.)

### §7.4 — Who can melee
Infantry, cavalry, camels and Dervish leaders attack. Everyone except gunboats defends.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my artillery melee attack?" (No.)
  - "Can the Maxims?" (No.)
  - "Can artillery defend in melee?" (Yes.)
  - "Can the Khalifa attack in melee?" (Yes.)
  - "Can Kitchener?" (No.)

### §7.5 — Cavalry and camel retreat
They may retreat two hexes from an infantry melee attack. One retreat per unit per turn. If they retreat next to enemies who haven't attacked yet, those enemies may attack them.
- *Questions:*
  - "Infantry charges my Lancers. Can I get away?" (Yes, retreat 2 hexes.)
  - "Can I retreat from a cavalry charge?" (Only from *infantry* melee.)
  - "My camels retreated and got attacked again. Can they retreat again?" (No, once per turn.)
  - "Does retreating happen before or instead of rolling?" (The manual does not say. See Part C.)

### §7.6 — Advance after melee
If all defenders are eliminated, the Dervish MUST advance with every surviving eligible unit (adjacent and took part), up to the stacking limit. The Anglo-Egyptian player may advance. Only attackers advance.
- *Questions:*
  - "My Hadendowa killed the defenders. Do I have to move in?" (Yes.)
  - "Can I leave one unit behind?" (No. All eligible units, up to the stacking limit.)
  - "Can my British battalions decline to advance?" (Yes.)
  - "Can the defender advance after a failed attack?" (No, only attackers.)
  - "Does a forced advance pull my units out of good terrain?" (Yes, it can: all eligible Dervish units must advance.)

### §7.7 — Resolving melee
Both sides roll on the CRT. Dervish +2, Anglo-Egyptian +1. No terrain modifiers except the Zariba. Losses come from the meleeing units first.
- *Questions:*
  - "Does the defender roll too?" (Yes, both roll.)
  - "Does hut terrain help me in melee?" (No.)
  - "What's the Dervish melee bonus?" (+2.)
  - "Losses: do I pick from the whole stack?" (From the units that took part in the melee first.)
  - "Which column do I use?" (Not stated; presumably the melee strength. See Part C.)
  - "Does the trench help defenders?" (Yes: Dervish get −2 instead of +2 against entrenched units, 9.232.)

## §8 Night

### §8.1 — Night effects
Anglo-Egyptian movement is halved, rounding down. No howitzers. All ranges are halved for both sides, rounding down, but range 1 stays 1. The multiplier at each range is unchanged. Example: rifles ×2 at 1, ×1 at 2, nothing at 3 or more.
- *Questions:*
  - "Is my Dervish movement halved at night?" (No, only the Anglo-Egyptian side.)
  - "My 15-MP cavalry at night?" (7.)
  - "Can my artillery fire at full range at night?" (No, it's halved.)
  - "Spears are range 1. Do they still work?" (Yes, range 1 stays 1.)
  - "Which turns are night?" (9, 10, 19 and 20, the shaded boxes.)
  - "Do Maxims get a second fire at night?" (Not prohibited, only howitzers are.)

### §8.2 — Dervish desertion
Once per campaign, on the first night turn (turn 9), in the movement phase: roll a d10, and 1½ × the roll in units desert. The Dervish player picks which, but not the Khalifa, gunboats, artillery or forts. No VP for deserters.
- *Questions:*
  - "When do my warriors run off?" (The first night turn, during movement.)
  - "I rolled a 4. How many go?" (6.)
  - "Can I choose to lose my worst units?" (Yes.)
  - "Can I lose a leader?" (Leaders other than the Khalifa aren't excluded.)
  - "Does the British player score for them?" (No.)
  - "Does it happen in Fall of Khartoum too?" ("Once each campaign game": no.)

### §10.11–10.14 — River mines
Secretly record 2 different Nile hexes south of the hexrow where the Khor Shambat meets the Nile. A British gunboat entering one stops and rolls a d10: 1–4 no effect, 5–7 damaged (drifts 2 hexes per turn downstream for the rest of the game; guns still work), 8–10 sunk. Only two rolls in total. Dervish gunboats are immune.
- *Questions:*
  - "My gunboat just stopped. What happened?" (A mine (10.12) or the river chain (10.22).)
  - "Can my damaged gunboat still shoot?" (Yes, until it drifts out of range.)
  - "Does a mine-sunk gunboat give the Dervish 10 VP?" (A gunboat sunk is 10 VP.)
  - "Can both mines be in one hex?" (No.)
  - "After both have gone off, are there more?" (No.)
  - "Can my Dervish gunboats sail through?" (Yes.)

### §10.21–10.23 — River chain
Secretly record a line of up to 4 river hexes south of the Khor Shambat hexrow. British gunboats entering one stop. No gunboat crosses it until the British sink it, either with an infantry or cavalry unit spending a whole turn adjacent on a bank, or with artillery scoring 3+.
- *Questions:*
  - "Can my Dervish gunboats cross the chain?" (No, nobody can.)
  - "How do I get rid of the chain?" (A whole turn adjacent on the bank, or artillery 3+.)
  - "Can a gunboat's own artillery shoot the chain?" (Only if gunboats count as "artillery", which the manual does not say. See Part C.)
  - "How long can the chain be?" (4 hexes.)

## Reference: Combat Results, Disruption, Turn Track

### Explanation of Combat Results
— = miss. D = ½ of the units in the target hex are disrupted (inverted), rounding up. # = that many units eliminated. Anglo-Egyptian direct fire +1, brigade integrity +1. Modified rolls below 1 count as 1, above 10 as 10.
- *Questions:*
  - "D on a stack of 3. How many flip?" (2.)
  - "The result is '2' but the hex has 1 unit. What happens?" (It's eliminated. See Part C for any excess.)
  - "My modifiers bring the roll to −1. Is that an automatic miss?" (No, treat it as 1.)
  - "Can I roll above 10 with bonuses?" (It caps at 10.)

### Disrupted units
No ZOC, no movement, no fire (offensive or defensive), no melee. They turn face up at the end of the owning player's turn.
- *Questions:*
  - "My unit got disrupted in his defensive fire. Can it melee this turn?" (No.)
  - "When does it recover?" (At the end of *your* player turn.)
  - "Can a disrupted unit defend in melee?" (It may not "melee". See Part C.)
  - "If my unit is disrupted during the enemy's turn, is it still disrupted for my next movement?" (It flips at the end of the owning player's turn, so it recovers at the end of your next turn, after sitting out your movement.)
  - "Can a disrupted unit hold the Tomb?" (No, the Tomb needs undisrupted units.)
  - "Can a disrupted unit be disrupted again?" (Not addressed.)

# Part C — Questions the Manual Leaves Open or Answers Only by Implication

These are questions a player could reasonably ask that the text doesn't answer directly. Each
note says what the text implies, where it implies anything.

1. **"Fired at once": per unit or per hex?** (§6.14) "A combat unit… may only be fired at once." If one unit in a stack was targeted, can the rest be targeted? The usual reading is that the hex can be attacked once.
2. **Desertion rounding** (§8.2): 1½ × an odd roll (e.g. 7 gives 10.5). Rounding isn't stated. Also, does a d10 "0" read as 10?
3. **Infantry melee destroys forts** (§6.54 cites 7.6, but 7.6 says nothing about forts). How does a melee kill a fort, and what happens to its garrison?
4. **Does the Anglo-Egyptian +1 apply to howitzer fire?** It applies to "all direct fire attacks", and howitzer fire is not direct.
5. **Howitzer combined with Maxims that scatters** (§6.42): do the Maxims still resolve against the intended hex, and how is the howitzer's separate CRT roll handled when combined?
6. **Do Friendlies get the Anglo-Egyptian +1 accuracy bonus?** They fire on the Dervish table but are Anglo-Egyptian units.
7. **Melee CRT column**: combined melee strength of all participating attackers? And the defender's column: total defending melee strength? What happens when one hex is attacked from several hexes?
8. **"Melee losses must be taken from meleeing units first"**: does that apply to the defender (whose whole stack defends) or only to attackers who also have non-participating stackmates?
9. **Cavalry retreat timing** (§7.5): declared before the dice? Does the attacker still roll? Can the attacker advance into the vacated hex? Can the retreat pass through or end in a ZOC?
10. **Can disrupted units defend in melee?** "May not melee" suggests they can't attack; §7.4 says all units defend.
11. **Excess eliminations**: a "3" against a hex holding 2 units. Does the excess carry over to anything?
12. **Disrupting leaders and forts**: can an Anglo-Egyptian leader be disrupted? (It matters for the Tomb condition.) Do forts disrupt?
13. **"British leader"** (§9.14): are Kitchener, Gatacre and Hunter all "British" for 10 VP and Tomb purposes? Presumably yes. Gordon isn't in the campaign.
14. **Royal Engineers timing**: they move adjacent in movement and must still be adjacent and undisrupted "at the end of the Anglo-Egyptian player turn". So demolition can finish on the same turn they arrive. Is that intended, compared with the Zariba's begin-and-end rule?
15. **Zariba builders' defensive fire**: forbidden or allowed? Only "fire offensively nor melee attack" is banned.
16. **Gunboat ZOC vs leaders/forts on islands**, and whether ZOC applies between gunboats separated by a chain.
17. **Overrun of Anglo-Egyptian leaders by an advance after combat**: does an advance count as "occupies"? Implied yes (Gordon's rule says so explicitly).
18. **Moving through enemy units**: not explicitly forbidden, but ZOC stops entry next to them first. Can a Dervish unit move through a hex holding only an Anglo-Egyptian leader? Yes (§6.51a).
19. **Night range halving at odd ranges**: range 3 halves to 1 (rounded down). Which multiplier applies at night range 1, the original range-1 multiplier? (§8.1 says yes, effects unchanged by range.)
20. **Mine hex "enter"**: does drifting into a mined hex trigger it? Does a gunboat already damaged by a mine trigger the second mine?
21. **Chain "one complete turn"**: a full game turn, or one Anglo-Egyptian player turn? Can it be disrupted mid-way?
22. **Historical scenario tallies**: do leaders count as "units eliminated"? Does the Khalifa count extra?
23. **Fall of Khartoum gunboat movement at night** (turn 1): halved, since gunboats are Anglo-Egyptian units?
24. **Brigade integrity with Friendlies** (are they a "brigade" for 5.54?). They aren't British, Sudanese or Egyptian infantry, so presumably no.
25. **Dervish fire at gunboats**: Dervish artillery, forts and gunboats can, since they are artillery. Can Dervish gunboats fire at British gunboats? Yes, the artillery line.
26. **Stacking during advance**: an advance can't overstack ("up to the stacking limit"). Do leaders count toward that limit?
27. **The Mahdi's Tomb inside the walled city**: an Anglo-Egyptian leader must go through a gate or breach. Do leaders count as units that "can get to the walled city"? Yes, per §5.23.
28. **Firing at a lone Anglo-Egyptian leader**: Kitchener alone in a hex has no combat values. Can he be targeted by fire at all, and does a hit eliminate him? §6.51 lists only overrun and the loss of his whole stack.
