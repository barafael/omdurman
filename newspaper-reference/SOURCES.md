What the front page borrows: ornate masthead over a double rule with issue /
date / price line (The Graphic, ILN); a headline across the page; the lead
article with stacked decks and a dateline ("OMDURMAN, Sept. 2.--", NYT 1890);
narrow justified columns with small-caps section heads (ILN); a column of
late telegrams beside the lead (the end screen's "Late War Bulletins").
